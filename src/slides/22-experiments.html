HOW DO THE ALGORITHMS WORK?
VDVertex Degree

Select the k vertices with the highest degree.

Planting the clique increases their degrees; the algorithm relies on this increase standing out from natural degree fluctuations.

CNCommon Neighbors

For an edge (i, j), examine its common neighbors.

If there are exactly k − 2 and they form a clique, recover those vertices together with i and j.

HCHarmonic Centrality

Rank vertices by their global proximity to the rest of the network, using shortest-path distances.

Convention: . Select the k highest-scoring vertices.
