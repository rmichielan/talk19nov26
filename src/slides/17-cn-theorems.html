V. COMMON NEIGHBORS

Recovery guarantees for CN

THEOREM 3.3POSITIVE RESULT

Common Neighbors achieves exact recovery w.h.p. if any of the following holds:

ALarge mean degree

Large mean degree rules out false positives, even for very small planted cliques.

BToo few common neighbors

Natural edges rarely have the small number of common neighbors required.

CToo many common neighbors required

The required number of common neighbors is too large for a natural edge.

Positive, sufficient conditions; depend on the dimension.
